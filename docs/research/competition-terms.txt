6.​   Finalist Selection: From eligible Submissions received during the Contest Period, Sponsor will select three (3) finalists
      (“Finalists”) based on (1) originality and quality of the prototype for agent-oriented software collaboration (50%), (2)
      effectiveness of multi-agent concurrency, coordination, context preservation, review, and conflict handling (25%), and
      (3) ease of use and product/user experience (25%), scored on a scale of 1–5 each. In the event of a tie, Finalists will be
      determined by the highest score in the “originality and quality of the prototype for agent-oriented software
      collaboration” category. Finalists will be notified in advance of Cloudflare Connect and will each have ten (10) minutes
      to present their project live on stage at Cloudflare Connect, Moscone West, San Francisco, California, on October 21,
      2026. Sponsor may provide reasonable travel and hotel accommodations and tickets to Cloudflare Connect for up to
      two (2) representatives per Finalist, as determined by Sponsor. Finalists are responsible for passports, visas, travel
      documents, incidentals, meals, personal expenses, and any costs not approved by Sponsor. Travel benefits are not
      transferable or redeemable for cash.
